College Capstone Project

About HealthAI

The “AI-Based Health Information Chatbot” is an academic prototype developed to demonstrate the responsible application of conversational interfaces in public health education.

Why This Project Was Created

In online spaces, users often search symptoms and encounter alarming, unverified, or misleading medical misinformation. This project was developed to provide a controlled, reliable educational interface that emphasizes safe self-care and encourages timely professional medical intervention.

How AI Empowers Health Information Access

Conversational agents allow users to query naturally using everyday language rather than complex clinical codes. By classifying inquiries and presenting structured information (overview, symptoms, self-care, warnings), AI reduces cognitive overload and improves comprehension.

System Limitations & Constraints
  • No Clinical Diagnostic Capability: The system cannot run medical tests, interpret patient history, or diagnose diseases.
  • No Drug Prescription: The system never prescribes pharmaceutical dosages or specific prescription medications.
  • No Real-time Emergency Monitoring: Not connected to dispatch services or live hospital triage infrastructure.
  • Prototype Dataset: Designed for demonstration and educational evaluation within academic project boundaries.
